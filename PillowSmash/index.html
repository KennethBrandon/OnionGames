THE RULES

Whack your opponent with your pillow until their health is gone. Knock them off the island and the LAVA does the rest! Best of 3 rounds. The lower your health, the farther you fly.

THE PILLOW SLAM

Your SLAM meter fills up over time and every time you land (or take) a hit. When it's full, slam your pillow on the ground for a huge power explosion that blasts your opponent away and takes out a big chunk of life!

CONTROLS

PLAYER 1
AD move   W jump   S block
F swing pillow   G PILLOW SLAM
PLAYER 2
←→ move   ↑ jump   ↓ block
K swing pillow   L PILLOW SLAM
Gamepads work too (stick/d-pad to move, A jump, X swing, B/Y slam). Hold block + jump to drop through a floating platform. Esc pauses. On phones, use the on-screen buttons.

TIPS

Some fighters shoot feathers, some have super long pillows, some have exploding pillows. Try them all!
